Mum,

I've been trying to think of what to write and it keeps coming back to the same thing. You are the most loving person I know. Not loving in a loud way. Loving in the way you show up, every single day, for everyone around you, and most of all for me.

You love God and you love Jesus, and you don't just say it. You live it. I've watched you pray through the hard seasons and thank Him through the good ones, and I've watched how you treat people when nobody is looking. That's the part that gets me. You live a pure Christian life without ever making a show of it.

You've been a great mother to me my whole life. The photo up there is proof. I was small and grinning and had no idea how much you were carrying that day, and you still knelt down to my level so I'd look good in the picture. That's you.

So much of who I am is copied from you. Your wisdom, the way you think before you act, how you handle things with grace. When I don't know what to do, I ask myself what you'd do, and usually that's the answer.

Thank you for everything. I love you.

Mofe
